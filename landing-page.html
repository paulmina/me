FAQ

The useful details, answered.

What services do you offer?+

Brand direction, visual identity, website design, front-end development, creative direction, and ongoing design support.

Do you work with people outside Melbourne?+

Yes. I work remotely with clients across Australia and internationally through email, video calls, and shared project files.

How long does a project take?+

Smaller design projects can take two to four weeks. A complete identity and website usually takes four to eight weeks, depending on scope.

Can you work with my existing brand?+

Yes. I can refine what is already working or create a new direction where the current system is holding the project back.

What is the first step?+

Send an email with a short description of your project, goals, and timeline. I will reply with the next practical step.
